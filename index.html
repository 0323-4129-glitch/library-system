Chapter one

What the system does

It replaces the sign-up sheet and the stopwatch. Every reservation, session, and remaining hour is kept in one record.

COMP 001

Computer reservations

Students pick an available computer and a time. Library staff approve or decline each request.

TIME 002

Library hours, tracked

A countdown shows remaining time at the computer, with a warning before a session ends.

DESK 003

Walk-ins welcome

A student without a reservation can use any available computer and sign in right away. No admin approval is needed.

REC 004

Records and reports

Usage history for students, and sign-in records and reports for the library.

Chapter three

How a visit works

From booking to the lock screen, in four steps.

  1. The student reserves

    They sign in to the student portal and choose a computer and a time.

  2. The library approves

    Staff review the reservation and the student is notified. Walk-ins skip this step and go straight to an available computer.

  3. They sign in at the computer

    The kiosk lock screen opens for their Student ID and access code, and the countdown begins.

  4. The session closes

    When time is up, or the library closes, the computer locks and the hours are recorded.

Library kiosk app for Windows

Installed on each student computer. It holds the screen on the sign-in page and runs the countdown. Library staff install it once per computer, with administrator rights.
